Geocaching

What is Geocaching?

Geocaching is a free real-world outdoor treasure hunt. Players try to locate hidden containers, called geocaches, using a smartphone or GPS and can then share their experiences online at Geocaching.com.

Where do you geocache?

We geocache where we live and occasionally when we vacation.

How often do you geocache?

We infrequently geocache at the moment. Times and locations will be posted on the website.
